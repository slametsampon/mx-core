# 🔷 LEVEL 1 – JUNIOR (0–2 Tahun)

_KURIKULUM 12 BULAN – TEKNISI JUNIOR E&I_

Baik. Berikut saya susun **Kurikulum Artikel Bulanan 1 Tahun untuk Teknisi Junior E&I (Electrical, Instrumentation & Control)** dengan pendekatan:

- **60% Troubleshooting & Practical Case**
- **25% Preventive Maintenance**
- **15% Basic Theory & Standard Awareness**
- Level: **Junior Technician (0–3 tahun pengalaman)**
- Fokus: Plant Petrokimia umum (rotating equipment, process unit, utility)

Struktur setiap bulan terdiri dari:

- 2 artikel troubleshooting
- 1 artikel preventive maintenance
- 1 artikel basic theory/standard awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 1 – Fundamental Electrical Safety & Motor Basic

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Motor LV trip saat start – langkah investigasi sistematis
- MCC breaker sering trip akibat overload vs short circuit

### 2. Preventive:

- Checklist inspeksi harian MCC & panel distribusi

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Dasar proteksi listrik & pengenalan ANSI relay code (50/51/27/59)

Referensi standar:

- NFPA
- IEEE

---

## 🔵 BULAN 2 – Induction Motor & Alignment Awareness

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Motor overheating – electrical atau mechanical?
- Vibrasi tinggi setelah coupling replacement

### 2. Preventive:

- Insulation Resistance Test & Polarization Index (PI)

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Prinsip kerja motor induksi 3 fasa

Referensi:

- IEEE 43 (Insulation Testing)

---

## 🔵 BULAN 3 – Basic Instrumentation (Pressure & Temperature)

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Pressure transmitter reading tidak stabil
- Temperature transmitter error setelah shutdown

### 2. Preventive:

- Praktik kalibrasi transmitter (bench vs loop test)

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Konsep 4–20 mA & loop wiring

Referensi:

- International Electrotechnical Commission

---

## 🔵 BULAN 4 – Control Valve & Pneumatic System

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Control valve hunting saat beban naik
- Valve tidak mencapai posisi 100%

### 2. Preventive:

- Air quality requirement (ISO 8573 awareness)
- Positioner calibration basic

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Prinsip kerja control valve & actuator

---

## 🔵 BULAN 5 – PLC & DCS Basic Awareness

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- PLC kehilangan komunikasi dengan field device
- Analog input reading tidak sesuai di DCS

### 2. Preventive:

- Backup & restore PLC program (awareness level)

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Perbedaan PLC vs DCS
- Basic redundancy concept

---

## 🔵 BULAN 6 – Transformer & Power Distribution

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Transformer overheating alarm
- Unbalance voltage pada panel distribusi

### 2. Preventive:

- Visual inspection & thermography awareness

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Basic transformer operation & protection

Referensi:

- IEEE C57

---

## 🔵 BULAN 7 – Analyzer & Sampling System Basic

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Gas analyzer reading drifting
- Moisture analyzer false alarm

### 2. Preventive:

- Cleaning & maintenance sampling line

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Prinsip kerja gas analyzer & GC basic

---

## 🔵 BULAN 8 – Safety Instrumented System (SIS) Awareness

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- ESD valve gagal close saat test
- False trip pada safety loop

### 2. Preventive:

- Proof test awareness & dokumentasi

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Perbedaan BPCS vs SIS
- Konsep dasar SIL

Referensi:

- IEC 61511

---

## 🔵 BULAN 9 – Shutdown Preparation

### 1. Troubleshooting Case:

- Temuan umum saat pre-shutdown inspection

### 2. Preventive:

- Penyusunan checklist shutdown E&I

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Risk assessment dasar (JSA)

---

## 🔵 BULAN 10 – Cable & Grounding System

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Ground fault pada motor feeder
- Signal noise pada analog instrument

### 2. Preventive:

- Inspection cable gland & termination

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Earthing & bonding system basic

Referensi:

- IEEE 80

---

## 🔵 BULAN 11 – Rotating Equipment Instrumentation

### 1. Troubleshooting:

- Vibration transmitter abnormal reading
- Bearing temperature spike

### 2. Preventive:

- Inspection vibration probe & RTD wiring

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Basic machinery protection system

---

## 🔵 BULAN 12 – Integrated Case Study & Review

### 1. Major Case Study:

- Motor trip → valve malfunction → plant upset (integrated E&I case)

### 2. Preventive:

- Review PM program effectiveness

### 3. Basic Theory:

- Root Cause Analysis (5 Why & Fishbone)

---

## 📊 Struktur Standar Setiap Artikel

Untuk konsistensi profesional, setiap artikel mengikuti format:

1. Background
2. Symptom / Finding
3. Possible Causes
4. Step-by-step Investigation
5. Reference Standard (jika relevan)
6. Root Cause
7. Corrective Action
8. Lesson Learned

---

## 🎯 Outcome Setelah 1 Tahun

Teknisi junior diharapkan mampu:

- Melakukan troubleshooting dasar secara sistematis
- Membaca single line diagram & loop diagram
- Memahami basic safety standard awareness
- Melakukan inspeksi rutin tanpa ketergantungan penuh pada senior
- Memahami interaksi Electrical–Instrument–Control

---

Fokus:

- Mengikuti SOP
- Basic troubleshooting
- PM execution
- Safety awareness

Output:

- Bisa bekerja mandiri pada pekerjaan rutin
- Mengerti risiko dasar
