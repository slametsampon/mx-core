Terima kasih.
Dengan metodologi yang sama (60% troubleshooting & case, 25% preventive/reliability, 15% theory & standard awareness), berikut saya susun **Roadmap Kurikulum 3 Level untuk Teknisi Mechanical** di plant petrokimia.

Fokus utama:

- Rotating Equipment (Pump, Compressor, Turbine)
- Static Equipment (Vessel, Heat Exchanger, Piping)
- Mechanical Integrity & Reliability
- Shutdown & Turnaround

---

# 🔷 LEVEL 1 – JUNIOR MECHANICAL (0–2 Tahun)

## Fokus:

- Eksekusi pekerjaan sesuai SOP
- Basic inspection & alignment
- Basic troubleshooting
- Safety & permit awareness

Komposisi:

- 60% Practical & troubleshooting
- 25% Preventive maintenance
- 15% Basic theory & code awareness

---

# 📘 KURIKULUM JUNIOR – 12 BULAN

---

## 🔵 BULAN 1 – Safety & Basic Rotating Equipment

Troubleshooting:

- Pompa tidak discharge setelah start
- Abnormal noise pada centrifugal pump

Preventive:

- Daily inspection checklist pump & coupling

Theory:

- Prinsip kerja centrifugal pump
- Basic API 610 awareness

Referensi:

- American Petroleum Institute API 610

---

## 🔵 BULAN 2 – Alignment & Coupling

Troubleshooting:

- Vibrasi tinggi setelah maintenance
- Bearing cepat rusak

Preventive:

- Soft foot checking
- Dial vs laser alignment basic

Theory:

- Thermal growth awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 3 – Mechanical Seal Basic

Troubleshooting:

- Seal leakage setelah start-up
- Dry running damage

Preventive:

- Flushing plan basic
- Seal pot inspection

Referensi:

- API 682 awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 4 – Bearing & Lubrication

Troubleshooting:

- Bearing overheating
- Grease over-lubrication case

Preventive:

- Grease interval calculation basic
- Oil level inspection

---

## 🔵 BULAN 5 – Reciprocating Compressor Basic

Troubleshooting:

- Compressor discharge temperature tinggi
- Valve failure symptoms

Preventive:

- Clearance measurement awareness

Referensi:

- API 618 awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 6 – Heat Exchanger & Static Equipment

Troubleshooting:

- Fouling impact pada performance
- Tube leakage symptom

Preventive:

- Visual inspection during shutdown

Referensi:

- ASME Section VIII awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 7 – Piping & Flange

Troubleshooting:

- Flange leakage setelah startup
- Gasket failure case

Preventive:

- Bolt tightening sequence awareness

Referensi:

- ASME B31.3 awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 8 – Valve Mechanical

Troubleshooting:

- Gate valve tidak bisa full close
- Stuck valve case

Preventive:

- Manual valve greasing & inspection

---

## 🔵 BULAN 9 – Shutdown Preparation

- Pre-TAR checklist
- Gasket & bolt preparation
- Spare readiness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 10 – Vibration Basic Awareness

Troubleshooting:

- Unbalance vs misalignment symptom

Theory:

- Basic vibration terminology

---

## 🔵 BULAN 11 – Lubrication System

Troubleshooting:

- Oil contamination case

Preventive:

- Oil sampling awareness

---

## 🔵 BULAN 12 – Integrated Case Study

Case:

- Pump failure → seal leak → bearing damage

✔ Bisa inspeksi pump
