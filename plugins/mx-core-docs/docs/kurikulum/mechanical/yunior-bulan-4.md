# 📘 ARTIKEL 1

## Bearing Overheating – Masalah Lubrikasi, Misalignment, atau Overload?

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

1. Judul: Bearing Overheating pada Centrifugal Pump
2. Disiplin: Mechanical
3. Level: Junior
4. Kategori: Troubleshooting
5. Equipment: Pump Bearing Housing (Grease / Oil Lubricated)
6. Referensi Standar:
   American Petroleum Institute – API 610 (Bearing Monitoring Awareness)

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective (Measurable & Skill-Based)

Setelah membaca artikel ini, teknisi mampu:

- LO1 – Mengidentifikasi minimal 5 penyebab bearing overheating
- LO2 – Mengukur dan membandingkan suhu bearing dengan baseline
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko keselamatan akibat overheat (fire hazard & rotating hazard)

---

## 3️⃣ System Context & Criticality

Motor → Coupling → Shaft → Bearing → Pump

Jika bearing overheating:

- Grease breakdown
- Shaft scoring
- Seal failure
- Shutdown unit

Interaksi lintas disiplin:

Bearing temperature ↑ → Vibration ↑ → Alarm (Instrument) → Trip motor (Electrical).

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section (WAJIB)

Teknisi harus mampu membaca:

- Posisi bearing pada assembly drawing
- Jalur gaya radial & axial
- Posisi grease nipple / oil sight glass

Harus mampu menunjukkan:

- Titik pengukuran temperatur
- Jalur pelumasan
- Titik isolasi pompa

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Normal bearing temperature: 65°C
Saat ini: 92°C

Vibration meningkat 20%.
Arus motor normal.

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Housing terasa sangat panas
- Grease discoloration

Terukur:

- Temperatur meningkat bertahap selama 1 minggu
- Axial vibration meningkat

Asumsi awal operator:

- Bearing defect kualitas buruk

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes (Structured Hypothesis)

A. Mechanical

- Misalignment
- Excessive axial load
- Bearing internal damage

B. Lubrication

- Grease kurang
- Grease over-lubrication
- Grease salah spesifikasi

C. Human Error

- Interval grease tidak sesuai

D. Process

- Pump overload

---

## 8️⃣ Step-by-Step Investigation Flow

1. Verifikasi suhu dengan thermometer
2. Cek interval grease terakhir
3. Periksa alignment
4. Cek axial vibration
5. Analisa warna & kondisi grease

Decision Point:
Lubrication diverifikasi sebelum membongkar bearing.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause & Contributing Factor

Root Cause:
Grease over-lubrication menyebabkan churning & overheating.

Contributing Factor:
Tidak ada perhitungan interval grease yang jelas.

---

## 🔟 Reference to Standard & Gap Analysis

API 610 menyarankan monitoring bearing temperature dan vibration.

Gap:

- Tidak ada baseline temperature record
- Tidak ada grease volume calculation

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective & Preventive Action

Immediate Action:

- Buang grease berlebih
- Set ulang volume grease

Permanent Fix:

- Hitung ulang interval & quantity grease

System Improvement:

- Buat lubrication chart

Monitoring Plan:

- Trend temperature & vibration

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

Potensi bahaya:

- Rotating hazard
- Grease ignition jika extreme temperature
- Slip hazard akibat grease spill

Permit:

- Mechanical work permit
- LOTO motor

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Interpretation & Trend Awareness

Parameter yang harus dipantau:

- Bearing temperature
- Axial & radial vibration
- Grease condition

Early Warning:

- Temperature naik gradual sebelum failure.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Skill Area: Bearing Troubleshooting
Level Saat Ini: W
Target Setelah Artikel: Menuju I

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question (Toolbox Use)

1. Mengapa terlalu banyak grease bisa berbahaya?
2. Apa perbedaan gejala misalignment vs over-lubrication?
3. Mengapa temperature trending penting?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway (Max 7 Bullet)

- Over-lubrication sama berbahayanya dengan kurang grease
- Temperatur dan vibration harus dianalisa bersama
- Baseline data wajib tersedia
- Jangan langsung menyalahkan bearing quality

# 📘 ARTIKEL 2

## Grease Over-Lubrication Case – Silent Damage yang Sering Terjadi

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

Disiplin: Mechanical
Level: Junior
Kategori: Troubleshooting

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective

- LO1 – Mengidentifikasi tanda over-lubrication
- LO2 – Menghitung volume grease dasar
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko pressure build-up pada housing

---

## 3️⃣ System Context

Grease berlebih → Churning → Heat → Seal damage → Bearing failure.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section

- Posisi grease nipple
- Relief port
- Bearing housing

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Teknisi menambahkan grease setiap minggu tanpa perhitungan.

Setelah 2 bulan:

- Bearing temperature meningkat
- Grease keluar dari seal

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Grease keluar berlebihan
- Seal rusak

Terukur:

- Temperature 15°C di atas normal

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes

A. Human Error

- Penambahan grease berlebihan

B. System

- Tidak ada lubrication chart

---

## 8️⃣ Investigation Flow

1. Periksa histori grease
2. Hitung kapasitas housing
3. Analisa grease condition

Decision Point:
Review data sebelum mengganti bearing.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause

Grease overfilling menyebabkan churning loss.

---

## 🔟 Gap Analysis

Tidak ada grease interval calculation.

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective Action

- Standardisasi grease quantity
- Training ulang teknisi

Monitoring:

- Temperature & grease purge volume

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

- Overpressure dapat merusak seal
- Slip hazard akibat grease spill

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Awareness

Trend temperature sebelum seal failure.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Lubrication discipline: W → I
