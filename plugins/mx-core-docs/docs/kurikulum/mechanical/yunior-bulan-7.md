# 📘 ARTIKEL 1

## Flange Leakage Setelah Startup – Torque Tidak Merata atau Gasket Damage?

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

1. Judul: Flange Leakage Pasca Startup – Investigasi Sistematis
2. Disiplin: Mechanical
3. Level: Junior
4. Kategori: Troubleshooting
5. Equipment: Process Piping Flange Joint (Class 150–600)
6. Referensi Standar: ASME B31.3 awareness

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective (Measurable & Skill-Based)

Setelah membaca artikel ini, teknisi mampu:

- LO1 – Mengidentifikasi penyebab kebocoran flange setelah startup
- LO2 – Membaca P&ID untuk menentukan jalur tekanan
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko keselamatan akibat pressurized leak

---

## 3️⃣ System Context & Criticality

Pump → Piping → Flange Joint → Downstream Equipment

Jika flange leak:

- Kehilangan produk
- Fire hazard (flammable service)
- Pressure drop
- Shutdown unit

Interaksi lintas disiplin:

Leak → Gas detector alarm (Instrument) → Interlock (Control) → Shutdown.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section (WAJIB)

Teknisi harus mampu membaca:

- P&ID jalur proses
- Posisi flange terhadap isolation valve
- Jalur tekanan upstream & downstream

Harus mampu menunjukkan:

- Titik vent & drain
- Arah aliran
- Lokasi blind / gasket

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Setelah startup unit:

- Terlihat drip pada flange discharge pump
- Tekanan operasi 12 bar
- Temperatur 120°C

Tidak ada leak saat hydrotest.

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Kebocoran kecil di satu sisi flange

Terukur:

- Tekanan stabil
- Temperatur normal

Asumsi awal operator:

- Gasket defect dari vendor

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes (Structured Hypothesis)

A. Mechanical

- Torque tidak merata
- Bolt elongation tidak seragam
- Gasket tidak center

B. Process

- Thermal expansion saat startup

C. Human Error

- Tidak mengikuti tightening sequence

D. System

- Misalignment piping menyebabkan stress

---

## 8️⃣ Step-by-Step Investigation Flow

1. Verifikasi service pressure & temperature
2. Cek histori tightening record
3. Periksa alignment piping
4. Evaluasi tightening sequence yang digunakan
5. Periksa ulang torque setelah pendinginan

Decision Point:
Piping stress diverifikasi sebelum mengganti gasket.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause & Contributing Factor

Root Cause:
Bolt tightening tidak mengikuti star pattern → uneven gasket compression.

Contributing Factor:
Tidak ada torque value record.

---

## 🔟 Reference to Standard & Gap Analysis

ASME B31.3 menekankan bahwa flange joint harus mampu menahan design pressure & temperature.

Gap:

- Tidak ada torque specification tertulis
- Tidak ada bolt tension verification

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective & Preventive Action

Immediate Action:

- Isolasi line & retighten sesuai sequence

Permanent Fix:

- Standardisasi torque chart

System Improvement:

- Training ulang tightening sequence

Monitoring Plan:

- Re-check torque setelah thermal cycle

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

Potensi bahaya:

- High pressure leak
- Fire/explosion risk
- Hot fluid exposure

Permit:

- Line isolation & depressurization
- Gas test sebelum pekerjaan

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Interpretation & Trend Awareness

Parameter penting:

- Pressure fluctuation saat startup
- Temperature ramp rate

Early Warning:

- Leak muncul setelah thermal expansion.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Skill Area: Flange Joint Troubleshooting
Level Saat Ini: W
Target Setelah Artikel: Menuju I

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question

1. Mengapa leak sering muncul saat startup, bukan saat hydrotest?
2. Apa dampak tightening tidak merata?
3. Mengapa piping alignment penting sebelum torque?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway

- Flange leak sering akibat prosedur tightening
- Thermal expansion mempengaruhi gasket compression
- Dokumentasi torque penting
- Piping stress dapat menyebabkan leak berulang

# 📘 ARTIKEL 2

## Gasket Failure Case – Salah Material atau Salah Instalasi?

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

Disiplin: Mechanical
Level: Junior
Kategori: Troubleshooting
Equipment: Spiral Wound / Non-Metallic Gasket

Referensi:
ASME – ASME B31.3 awareness

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective

- LO1 – Mengidentifikasi pola kegagalan gasket
- LO2 – Memahami hubungan gasket material dengan service condition
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko kebocoran pada service kritis

---

## 3️⃣ System Context

Gasket → Seal integrity → Process containment.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section

- Cross-section flange–gasket–bolt
- Posisi gasket pada raised face flange

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Gasket gagal dalam 2 minggu operasi.

Terlihat:

- Blow-out pada satu sisi
- Bolt masih utuh

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Gasket extrusion

Terukur:

- Tekanan operasi sesuai design

Asumsi awal:

- Material gasket tidak sesuai

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes

A. Mechanical

- Over-torque
- Under-torque
- Flange face damage

B. Process

- Pressure spike

C. Human

- Salah penyimpanan gasket

---

## 8️⃣ Investigation Flow

1. Periksa kondisi flange face
2. Review torque value
3. Evaluasi material gasket vs temperature
4. Periksa tightening pattern

Decision Point:
Verifikasi torque sebelum menyalahkan material.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause

Under-torque menyebabkan blow-out saat pressure spike.

---

## 🔟 Gap Analysis

Tidak ada torque wrench calibration record.

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective Action

- Gunakan torque wrench terkalibrasi
- Standardisasi gasket storage

Monitoring:

- Leak inspection rutin

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk Reflection

- Sudden gasket blow-out berisiko tinggi
- High pressure jet release

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Awareness

Leak muncul saat pressure surge.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Gasket diagnosis: W → I

---

# 📘 ARTIKEL 3

## Bolt Tightening Sequence Awareness – Mengapa Pola & Tahapan Penting?

(Kategori: Preventive)

Struktur tetap mengikuti 16 section.

Fokus:

- Star pattern tightening
- Multi-step torque (30%–60%–100%)
- Cross tightening
- Re-torque setelah thermal cycle

Safety:

- Pastikan line depressurized
- Gunakan torque wrench sesuai rating
