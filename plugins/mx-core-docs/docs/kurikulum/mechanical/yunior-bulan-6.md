# 📘 ARTIKEL 19

## Fouling pada Heat Exchanger – Dampak pada Performance & Overload System

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

1. Judul: Fouling Heat Exchanger dan Penurunan Performance
2. Disiplin: Mechanical
3. Level: Junior
4. Kategori: Troubleshooting
5. Equipment: Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger
6. Referensi Standar: ASME Section VIII awareness

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective (Measurable & Skill-Based)

Setelah membaca artikel ini, teknisi mampu:

- LO1 – Mengidentifikasi gejala fouling berdasarkan parameter operasi
- LO2 – Membaca P&ID heat exchanger dan jalur aliran fluida
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko tekanan berlebih akibat fouling

---

## 3️⃣ System Context & Criticality

Hot fluid → Heat Exchanger → Cooled fluid → Downstream process

Jika fouling terjadi:

- Heat transfer menurun
- Outlet temperature tidak tercapai
- Pressure drop meningkat
- Beban pompa meningkat

Interaksi lintas disiplin:

Fouling ↑ → Flow restriction ↑ → Motor load ↑ → Alarm electrical.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section (WAJIB)

Teknisi harus mampu membaca:

- P&ID jalur shell side & tube side
- Posisi inlet & outlet temperature indicator
- Jalur isolasi valve

Harus mampu menunjukkan:

- Titik pressure drop
- Titik vent & drain
- Arah aliran

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Normal outlet temperature: 60°C
Saat ini: 72°C

Pressure drop meningkat 30%.
Flow tetap.

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Tidak ada kebocoran eksternal

Terukur:

- Differential pressure meningkat
- Motor pump arus naik sedikit

Asumsi awal operator:

- Cooling water tidak cukup dingin

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes (Structured Hypothesis)

A. Mechanical

- Fouling pada tube
- Scaling

B. Process

- Kualitas fluida berubah

C. Instrument

- Temperature sensor drift

D. Human Error

- Tidak ada cleaning schedule

---

## 8️⃣ Step-by-Step Investigation Flow

1. Verifikasi temperatur aktual dengan alat portable
2. Hitung pressure drop aktual
3. Bandingkan data historis
4. Evaluasi jadwal cleaning terakhir
5. Inspeksi saat shutdown

Decision Point:
Data temperatur diverifikasi sebelum menyimpulkan fouling.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause & Contributing Factor

Root Cause:
Scaling pada tube side menyebabkan penurunan heat transfer.

Contributing Factor:
Tidak ada monitoring differential pressure rutin.

---

## 🔟 Reference to Standard & Gap Analysis

ASME Section VIII mengatur desain pressure vessel agar mampu menahan tekanan desain.

Gap:

- Operasi mendekati pressure design limit akibat fouling
- Tidak ada trend pressure drop

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective & Preventive Action

Immediate Action:

- Schedule cleaning

Permanent Fix:

- Implement DP monitoring

System Improvement:

- Buat cleaning interval berbasis data

Monitoring Plan:

- Trend outlet temperature & pressure drop

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

Potensi bahaya:

- Overpressure
- Rupture risk
- Hot surface

Permit:

- Confined space permit saat membuka shell
- Depressurization wajib

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Interpretation & Trend Awareness

Parameter penting:

- Outlet temperature
- Differential pressure
- Flow rate

Early Warning:

- Kenaikan DP gradual sebelum performance drop signifikan.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Skill Area: Heat Exchanger Troubleshooting
Level Saat Ini: W
Target Setelah Artikel: Menuju I

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question

1. Mengapa fouling meningkatkan beban pompa?
2. Apa risiko jika pressure drop diabaikan?
3. Mengapa trend data lebih penting daripada inspeksi visual saja?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway

- Fouling menurunkan efisiensi heat transfer
- Differential pressure adalah indikator utama
- Cleaning harus berbasis data
- Overpressure adalah risiko utama

# 📘 ARTIKEL 20

## Tube Leakage Symptom – Bagaimana Mendeteksi Sebelum Failure Besar?

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

Disiplin: Mechanical
Level: Junior
Kategori: Troubleshooting
Equipment: Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger

Referensi:
ASME – ASME Section VIII awareness

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective

- LO1 – Mengidentifikasi gejala tube leakage
- LO2 – Memahami perbedaan leak internal vs eksternal
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko kontaminasi fluida

---

## 3️⃣ System Context

Tube leak → Cross contamination → Product off-spec → Shutdown.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section

- Posisi tube bundle
- Jalur shell & tube side
- Titik vent & drain

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Operator menemukan:

- Pressure shell side turun
- Level downstream naik
- Tidak ada leak eksternal

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Tidak ada drip di flange

Terukur:

- Komposisi fluida berubah
- Pressure imbalance

Asumsi awal:

- Instrument error

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes

A. Mechanical

- Tube crack
- Corrosion

B. Process

- Overpressure

C. Human

- Tidak dilakukan inspection saat shutdown

---

## 8️⃣ Investigation Flow

1. Verifikasi komposisi fluida
2. Bandingkan pressure kedua sisi
3. Lakukan hydrotest saat shutdown
4. Lakukan tube plugging jika perlu

Decision Point:
Validasi data komposisi sebelum membongkar exchanger.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause

Tube corrosion akibat fouling jangka panjang.

---

## 🔟 Gap Analysis

Tidak ada thickness monitoring periodik.

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective Action

- Plugging tube rusak
- Implement corrosion monitoring

Monitoring:

- Pressure balance
- Chemical treatment review

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk Reflection

- Cross contamination
- Overpressure
- Chemical exposure

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Awareness

Trend pressure imbalance sebelum leak terdeteksi.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Static equipment diagnosis: W → I
