# 📘 ARTIKEL 1

## Pompa Tidak Discharge Setelah Start – Investigasi Sistematis Berbasis P&ID

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

Disiplin: Mechanical
Level: Junior
Kategori: Troubleshooting
Equipment: Centrifugal Pump – Utility / Process Service
Referensi: API 610 awareness

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective

Setelah membaca artikel ini, teknisi mampu:

- Mengidentifikasi minimal 5 penyebab pompa tidak discharge
- Membaca alur suction–discharge pada P&ID
- Menjelaskan hubungan antara priming dan head

---

## 3️⃣ System Context & Criticality

Pompa → Mengalirkan fluida → Menjaga flow ke control valve → Menjaga pressure system.

Jika pompa tidak discharge:

- Flow nol
- Level naik/turun abnormal
- Interlock low flow bisa aktif

👉 Interaksi Mechanical–Instrument–Control.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section (WAJIB)

Menggunakan:

- P&ID untuk melihat suction valve, discharge valve, check valve
- Pump curve vs system curve (basic awareness)

Teknisi harus bisa menunjukkan:

- Jalur fluida
- Titik isolasi
- Titik pressure gauge

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Pompa start normal.
Motor ampere normal.
Namun discharge pressure = 0 bar.

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Flow indicator menunjukkan nol

Terukur:

- Ampere normal
- Suction pressure rendah

Asumsi operator:

- Pompa rusak

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes (Structured)

Mechanical:

- Pump belum priming
- Impeller rusak
- Shaft tidak terhubung

Process:

- Suction valve tertutup
- Air pocket

Instrument:

- Pressure gauge error

Human:

- Valve lineup salah setelah maintenance

---

## 8️⃣ Step-by-Step Investigation

1. Verifikasi valve lineup di P&ID
2. Cek priming condition
3. Cek suction pressure
4. Dengarkan cavitation noise
5. Buka casing jika diperlukan

Decision logic:
Selalu verifikasi kondisi sistem sebelum bongkar pompa.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause & Contributing Factor

Root cause:
Suction valve belum dibuka penuh.

Contributing:
Tidak ada cross-check lineup setelah pekerjaan.

---

## 🔟 Reference Standard & Gap Analysis

API 610 menekankan:
Pump harus dioperasikan sesuai design suction condition.

Gap:
Tidak ada checklist pre-start alignment & valve verification.

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective & Preventive Action

Immediate:

- Buka suction valve penuh

Permanent:

- Buat checklist start-up

Monitoring:

- Monitor suction pressure sebelum start

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

- Risiko dry running → seal damage
- Risiko buka casing tanpa depressurize

Permit & LOTO wajib sebelum pembongkaran.

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Interpretation & Trend Awareness

Early warning:
Suction pressure turun 2 hari sebelumnya.

Parameter monitoring:

- Suction pressure
- Vibration
- Discharge pressure

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Pump troubleshooting:
W → Target I

P&ID reading:
A → W

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question

1. Mengapa ampere normal belum tentu pompa bekerja?
2. Apa hubungan NPSH dengan suction pressure?
3. Apa risiko dry running terhadap seal?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway

- Selalu baca P&ID sebelum investigasi
- 50% masalah adalah lineup system
- Jangan langsung bongkar pompa

# 📘 ARTIKEL 2

## Abnormal Noise pada Centrifugal Pump – Identifikasi Sumber Getaran & Kavitasi

---

## System Context

Pompa → Getaran → Bearing damage → Seal leak → Trip motor → Process upset.

---

## Diagram Literacy

- Pump cross-section (impeller, volute, shaft)
- Jalur fluida suction–discharge

---

## Failure Scenario

Noise seperti “kerikil” saat flow tinggi.
Vibration meningkat.

---

## Possible Causes

Mechanical:

- Bearing defect
- Misalignment

Hydraulic:

- Cavitation
- Recirculation

Process:

- Low suction head

---

## Investigation

1. Cek suction pressure
2. Cek vibration reading
3. Cek alignment
4. Bandingkan dengan pump curve

---

## Root Cause

Low NPSH akibat suction line restriction.

---

## Risk

Cavitation prolonged → impeller erosion.

---

Outcome diperkuat:
Analisa berbasis data & sistem.

# 📘 ARTIKEL 3

## Daily Inspection Checklist Pump & Coupling Berbasis Risk

---

## Learning Objective

- Melakukan inspeksi visual harian
- Mengidentifikasi early abnormality

---

## System Context

Loose coupling → misalignment → bearing damage → unplanned shutdown.

---

## Diagram Literacy

- Posisi coupling
- Arah rotation

---

## Checklist Outline

1. Visual leak check
2. Noise
3. Vibration feel
4. Oil level
5. Coupling guard condition
6. Foundation bolt tightness

---

## Risk

- Rotating hazard
- Guard removal without permit

---

## Trend Awareness

Record vibration manually (basic awareness).

---

Outcome diperkuat:
Inspeksi mandiri & safety.

---

## System Context

Pump adalah equipment critical service.
API 610 memastikan reliability & mechanical integrity.

---

## Diagram Literacy

- Pump curve basic
- Head vs flow

---

## Basic API 610 Awareness

- Minimum design margin
- Bearing life requirement
- Seal chamber design

---

## Failure Scenario Illustration

Pump non-API sering mengalami bearing failure dini.

---

## Risk

---
