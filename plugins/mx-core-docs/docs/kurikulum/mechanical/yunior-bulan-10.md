# 📘 ARTIKEL 31

## Unbalance vs Misalignment – Bagaimana Membedakan dari Gejala Vibrasi?

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

1. Judul: Unbalance vs Misalignment – Diagnosis Dasar Vibrasi
2. Disiplin: Mechanical
3. Level: Junior
4. Kategori: Troubleshooting
5. Equipment: Centrifugal Pump + Motor
6. Referensi Standar:
   American Petroleum Institute – API 610 (Vibration Monitoring Awareness)

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective (Measurable & Skill-Based)

Setelah membaca artikel ini, teknisi mampu:

- LO1 – Membedakan gejala unbalance dan misalignment berdasarkan data vibrasi
- LO2 – Membaca lokasi pengukuran vibrasi pada pump & motor
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko keselamatan akibat vibrasi tinggi (rotating hazard & structural looseness)

---

## 3️⃣ System Context & Criticality

Motor → Coupling → Pump Shaft → Bearing → Foundation

Jika vibrasi tinggi:

- Bearing life menurun
- Seal rusak
- Bolt loosen
- Struktur retak

Interaksi lintas disiplin:

Vibrasi ↑ → Temperature ↑ → Alarm (Instrument) → Trip (Electrical).

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section (WAJIB)

Teknisi harus mampu memahami:

- Posisi radial & axial measurement point
- Lokasi bearing housing
- Posisi coupling

Harus mampu menunjukkan:

- Arah horizontal / vertical / axial
- Lokasi misalignment potential

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Normal vibrasi: 2.8 mm/s
Saat ini: 6.5 mm/s

Axial vibration meningkat signifikan.

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Noise ringan
- Tidak ada leakage

Terukur:

- Axial vibration tinggi
- Radial vibration sedang

Asumsi awal:

- Impeller unbalance

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes (Structured Hypothesis)

A. Mechanical

- Unbalance
- Angular misalignment
- Parallel misalignment
- Soft foot

B. Process

- Cavitation

C. Human

- Alignment tidak diverifikasi

---

## 8️⃣ Step-by-Step Investigation Flow

1. Identifikasi arah vibrasi dominan
2. Bandingkan axial vs radial
3. Periksa histori alignment
4. Lakukan dial alignment check
5. Evaluasi impeller jika perlu

Decision Point:
Axial vibration tinggi → cek alignment dulu sebelum impeller.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause & Contributing Factor

Root Cause:
Angular misalignment.

Contributing Factor:
Tidak mempertimbangkan thermal growth.

---

## 🔟 Reference to Standard & Gap Analysis

API 610 menyarankan monitoring vibrasi pada titik bearing.

Gap:

- Tidak ada baseline axial vibration
- Tidak ada post-maintenance alignment record

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective & Preventive Action

Immediate Action:

- Realignment shaft

Permanent Fix:

- Implement alignment verification sheet

System Improvement:

- Vibration trending review setiap minggu

Monitoring Plan:

- Axial & radial trending

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

Potensi bahaya:

- Bolt fatigue
- Rotating hazard
- Structural looseness

Permit:

- LOTO sebelum membuka guard

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Interpretation & Trend Awareness

Unbalance:

- Dominan radial vibration
- Stabil terhadap beban

Misalignment:

- Axial vibration signifikan
- Temperature bearing naik

Early Warning:

- Axial vibration naik perlahan setelah maintenance.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Skill Area: Basic Vibration Interpretation
Level Saat Ini: A
Target Setelah Artikel: W

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question

1. Mengapa axial vibration sering terkait misalignment?
2. Bagaimana membedakan cavitation dengan unbalance?
3. Mengapa baseline data penting?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway

- Unbalance dominan radial
- Misalignment dominan axial
- Trending lebih penting dari satu data
- Alignment harus diverifikasi pasca maintenance

# 📘 ARTIKEL 32

## Basic Vibration Terminology – Bahasa Dasar yang Harus Dipahami Teknisi

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

Disiplin: Mechanical
Level: Junior
Kategori: Basic Theory
Equipment: Rotating Equipment

Referensi:
American Petroleum Institute – API 610 awareness

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective

- LO1 – Menjelaskan istilah amplitude, frequency, dan velocity
- LO2 – Memahami satuan mm/s dan µm
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko jika nilai vibrasi melebihi batas

---

## 3️⃣ System Context

Vibration monitoring → Early detection → Prevent failure.

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section

- Arah pengukuran (H, V, A)
- Grafik sederhana amplitude vs time

---

## 5️⃣ Basic Theory

Amplitude:
Besarnya getaran.

Frequency:
Jumlah siklus per detik (Hz).

Velocity (mm/s):
Parameter umum untuk rotating equipment.

---

## 6️⃣ Failure Illustration

Jika vibrasi > 7 mm/s:

- Bearing life menurun
- Structural fatigue meningkat

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Misinterpretation

- Menganggap angka besar selalu berarti unbalance
- Tidak mempertimbangkan arah vibrasi

---

## 8️⃣ Investigation Awareness

Satu titik tidak cukup – minimal 3 arah pengukuran.

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause Example

Interpretasi salah karena tidak memahami istilah dasar.

---

## 🔟 Gap Analysis

Tidak ada pelatihan vibration terminology formal.

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective Action

- Training dasar vibration
- Standarisasi reporting format

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk Reflection

- Salah interpretasi → keputusan salah
- Membongkar equipment tanpa perlu

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Awareness

Trend > snapshot.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Vibration terminology: A → W

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question

1. Mengapa velocity lebih umum digunakan?
2. Apa beda amplitude dan frequency?
3. Mengapa arah pengukuran penting?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway

- Pahami istilah sebelum menganalisa
- Axial vs radial penting
- Trend adalah alat utama
- Data tanpa pemahaman = risiko
