# 📘 ARTIKEL 35

## Pump Failure → Seal Leak → Bearing Damage

### (Integrated Mechanical Case – System Thinking Exercise)

---

## 1️⃣ Informasi Umum

1. Judul: Integrated Failure Case – Dari Misalignment hingga Bearing Failure
2. Disiplin: Mechanical
3. Level: Junior
4. Kategori: Integrated Troubleshooting / System Interaction
5. Equipment: Centrifugal Pump + Mechanical Seal + Bearing Housing
6. Referensi Standar:
   American Petroleum Institute – API 610 (Pump)
   American Petroleum Institute – API 682 (Mechanical Seal Awareness)

---

## 2️⃣ Learning Objective (Measurable & Skill-Based)

Setelah membaca artikel ini, teknisi mampu:

- LO1 – Menyusun kronologi kegagalan berbasis data
- LO2 – Mengidentifikasi hubungan alignment–seal–bearing secara sistem
- LO3 – Mengidentifikasi risiko keselamatan akibat seal leak dan overheating

---

## 3️⃣ System Context & Criticality

Motor → Coupling → Pump Shaft → Mechanical Seal → Bearing → Process Flow

Jika satu komponen gagal:

- Seal leak
- Lubrication contamination
- Bearing overheating
- Shutdown unit

Interaksi lintas disiplin:

Mechanical failure → Vibration ↑ → Temperature ↑ → Alarm (Instrument) → Trip (Electrical).

---

## 4️⃣ Diagram Literacy Section (WAJIB)

Teknisi harus mampu membaca:

- P&ID pump & seal flushing
- Posisi bearing housing
- Posisi coupling

Harus mampu menunjukkan:

- Jalur gaya akibat misalignment
- Jalur oil lubrication
- Titik seal leakage

---

## 5️⃣ Background & Failure Scenario

Data 3 minggu sebelum failure:

- Axial vibration naik dari 2.5 ke 4.8 mm/s
- Seal leakage kecil mulai terlihat
- Bearing temperature naik 8°C

1 minggu sebelum failure:

- Vibration 6.5 mm/s
- Leakage meningkat
- Oil mulai berubah warna

Hari kejadian:

- Bearing temperature 98°C
- Noise abnormal
- Pump trip otomatis

---

## 6️⃣ Symptom & Initial Finding

Terlihat:

- Seal leak signifikan
- Oil keruh
- Bearing housing sangat panas

Terukur:

- Axial vibration tinggi
- Radial vibration sedang
- Arus motor naik

Asumsi awal:

- Bearing defect kualitas buruk

---

## 7️⃣ Possible Causes (Structured Hypothesis)

A. Mechanical

- Misalignment
- Bearing defect
- Seal face wear

B. Lubrication

- Oil contamination

C. Process

- Pump overload

D. Human

- Tidak menindaklanjuti early leakage

---

## 8️⃣ Step-by-Step Investigation Flow

1. Review histori vibration
2. Review histori alignment terakhir
3. Periksa kondisi seal face
4. Analisa oil condition
5. Periksa bearing damage pattern

Decision Point:
Mulai dari data paling awal (vibration naik), bukan dari efek akhir (bearing rusak).

---

## 9️⃣ Root Cause & Contributing Factor

Root Cause Teknis:
Angular misalignment menyebabkan axial load berlebih → seal face wear → leakage → oil contamination → bearing overheating → failure.

Contributing Factor:

- Tidak ada review vibration trend
- Leakage kecil diabaikan
- Tidak ada oil sampling program

---

## 🔟 Reference to Standard & Gap Analysis

API 610:

- Alignment harus dalam tolerance
- Monitoring vibration wajib

API 682:

- Seal leakage harus dianalisa segera

Gap:

- Tidak ada post-maintenance alignment verification
- Tidak ada escalation ketika seal leak kecil muncul

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Corrective & Preventive Action

Immediate Action:

- Replace bearing & seal
- Realign pump

Permanent Fix:

- Alignment verification sheet mandatory
- Implement seal leakage threshold policy

System Improvement:

- Weekly vibration trend review
- Oil sampling program formal

Monitoring Plan:

- Axial vibration baseline
- Seal leakage log

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Risk & Safety Reflection

Potensi bahaya:

- Flammable fluid leak
- Bearing seizure → shaft lock
- Rotating hazard

Permit:

- LOTO sebelum pembongkaran
- Gas test jika fluida berbahaya

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Data Interpretation & Trend Awareness

Urutan kegagalan (Failure Chain):

1. Axial vibration naik (early warning)
2. Seal leakage muncul
3. Oil berubah warna
4. Bearing temperature naik
5. Failure total

Pelajaran:

Failure jarang terjadi tiba-tiba.
Selalu ada indikator awal.

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Competency Mapping

Skill Area: Integrated Troubleshooting
Level Saat Ini: W
Target Setelah Artikel: Mendekati I

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Discussion Question (Toolbox Use)

1. Jika seal leak kecil muncul, apa tindakan pertama?
2. Mengapa axial vibration adalah indikator penting?
3. Apa indikator paling awal yang terlewat?
4. Bagaimana mencegah failure chain seperti ini?

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Key Takeaway (Max 7 Bullet)

- Failure adalah rantai, bukan satu kejadian
- Axial vibration adalah early indicator misalignment
- Seal leak kecil tidak boleh diabaikan
- Oil contamination mempercepat bearing damage
- Data trend harus ditinjau rutin
- Alignment adalah fondasi reliability
- Preventive lebih murah daripada overhaul
